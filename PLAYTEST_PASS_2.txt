HOLY HELICOPTER - PLAYTEST PASS 2

IMPLEMENTED
Thrust-limited flight, momentum through turns, deliberate braking, angular yaw
acceleration, cockpit pitch/bank/acceleration lag and engine-load rotor audio.
Weapons retain a stabilized aim solution. ESC exposes a cockpit-motion slider.

A 2.2 km parish with three villages, river crossings, wooded ridges and remote
crypts. G opens the navigation chart; click destinations/supplies to set a course.
Eighteen civilians, staged shelter, village occupation, reclaiming and field aid.
Deliver eight people and four physical crypt seals. Seals use no passenger seats.

Crypt sentinels fire interruptible predictive salvos. Gargoyles pursue faster than
cruise, warn audibly, lock a committed attack path, then recover at a readable
distance. Destroying crypts provokes retaliation, including at the population cap.
Existing weapons, trigger assignments, crowd destruction, supplies, friendly fire,
towers, boarding, base service and session upgrades remain integrated.

REMAINING LIMITS
Human playtesting still needs to judge handling comfort, warning readability and
whether the encounter rhythm stays enjoyable. The opposition has meaningful roles
but limited attack variety. Ground navigation, shelter and village damage remain
lightweight abstractions. One tower at a time; no general scenery collision or
universal destruction, and no persistent campaign. No further implementation is pending.
