Reproducibility checks compare identical seeds after independent worlds are interleaved, and compare one batch against individual steps. Invariant checks sample every 32 ticks and the final tick, covering bounded histories, finite and bounded state, capacity, geography, causal order, and dated beliefs. Targeted mechanism checks are separate from these observational runs.
Seed diversity is checked using measured movement, conversations, gifts, other-owned acquisitions, corrections, survival, and directional asymmetry; a seed-containing state digest alone is not considered evidence of behavioral diversity.

`metrics.csv` includes first/last quarter activity, resource accounting, final state digests, and continuous relationship summaries. Positive/negative directions mean affinity above +0.15/below -0.15; attached directions mean attachment above 0.3; asymmetric pairs differ by more than 0.1 in trust or affinity. These diagnostic thresholds are not behavior triggers.
`action-counts.csv` lists recorded counts for every primitive, including zero-count actions, for each scenario and seed.
Cache diagnostics count actual completed events. An immediate cache reversal is an acquisition followed by a deposit (or vice versa) at the same personally owned cache within two ticks; this is a churn diagnostic, not a behavior prohibition.

Trace seed 19 is archived through tick 2000 when that seed is in the baseline list. `trace-events.csv` contains every retained event collected each tick in that interval, `trace-decisions.csv` contains completed decision snapshots and candidate reasons, and `trace-relations.csv` samples directional relationships each 100 ticks. Event cause IDs identify explicit prior experiences or information sources; they are a partial causal record, not a proof that the linked event was necessary.
`trace-relation-changes.csv` records before/after relationship snapshots when LastEventId changes. Passive decay alone is omitted. These are end-of-tick deltas: multiple interactions during one tick may contribute, and LastEventId is the most recent recorded cause. Blank before-values mean the relation was first seen during that tick.

Scarcity changes abundance to 0.35, regrowth to 0.45, and need rate to 1.25. Abundance changes abundance to 1.8 and regrowth to 1.5. Noisy information changes communication accuracy to 0.2. Small world uses 16x12, 12 inhabitants, and history capacities 64/4/2. Other settings are defaults. A single matched stress seed does not establish a general effect.

Unity scene import, rendering, pointer input, scaling, and live performance remain runtime checks. No Unity launch or Play Mode occurred during this validation.
