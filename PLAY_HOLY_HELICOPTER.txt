HOLY HELICOPTER - OPERATION MIDNIGHT MASS / PLAYTEST PASS 1

LAUNCH
Run Builds/HolyHelicopter/Holy Helicopter.exe. Keep its build folder together.
Or open Assets/Scenes/HolyHelicopter.unity in Unity 6000.4.1f1 and press Play.
No additional packages, paid assets or services are required.

YOUR FIRST SORTIE
Press Enter or BEGIN THE EXORCISM. Fly into the valley and follow the roads.
Start with silver on LMB and holy water on RMB. Water clears ordinary crowds.
The invasion starts with 380 monsters and builds toward thousands in 3-5 minutes.
Four remote crypts continuously produce reinforcements. Sealing them matters.
Stone crypts resist bullets and water; rockets make short work of their masonry.
Look for mint person symbols and waving people in orange safety vests.
Slow below 46 ft within 16 metres to winch a civilian. Four passenger seats.
Civilians flee actual monsters, seek safer paths, and approach a safe hovering heli.
Carry at least four people home if possible. Casualties reduce the mission rating;
a disastrous rescue effort no longer abruptly ends your flight.
Seal all crypts and deliver the attainable rescue quota, then return home to finish.

CONTROLS
WASD         Move relative to helicopter heading
Mouse        Turn / aim vertically, independently of body tilt
Q / E        Descend / ascend
Wheel        Change held altitude
LMB / RMB    Fire assigned weapons, including both together
Tab          Free cursor / return to flight
Click an unlocked icon with LMB or RMB to assign that trigger
Escape       Pause; sensitivity, invert-Y and audio settings
H            Field manual
M            Mute / unmute
R            Restart after victory or defeat
F            Reserved

SUPPLY RUNS AND UPGRADES
Fly below 46 ft across cargo boxes or fuel drums to hook them automatically.
You can keep moving at full flight speed after the hook latches.
Supplies refill 55% ammunition for owned weapons, 45 fuel, 40 hull or 35 salvage.
Full-resource crates stay put. Enemy supply drops are limited and need-biased.
Return to the gold Saint Rotor helipad below 36 ft and slow down for free service.
Press Tab at home to open the armory. Every unlocked weapon stays available.
Ordinary kills pay one salvage per eight; necromancers pay two extra.
Crypts pay 100. Delivered civilians pay 45 each.
Rockets cost 260, UV costs 420, Rapture costs 550. Upgrades compete for salvage.
Full fuel lasts about four minutes at continuous cruise, longer while hovering.

THREATS AND CONSEQUENCES
Moving or climbing shakes boarders off. Low stationary hovering invites towers.
Shoot a tower to trigger a bone avalanche, or move away before it reaches you.
Bats telegraph diving attacks. Necromancers charge and fire actual traveling hexes;
change direction after the warning, or kill the caster during its charge.
Altitude protects against ordinary ground melee; specialized enemies can reach you.
Orange reticle warnings flag civilians inside your selected rocket/shockwave blast.
Friendly fire remains lethal near a rocket impact. Water is safer, not harmless.
Watch rescue guidance for altitude, speed, range and full-cabin instructions.

WEAPONS
Silver: 190 m, rapid precision fire; particularly good against vampires and bats.
Water: 120 m, broad splash; particularly good against skeletons and zombies.
Rockets: 245 m, huge explosive crowd and crypt damage; dangerous to civilians.
UV: 160 m, wide violet beam; useful against flying monsters and vampires.
Rapture: ground shockwave below the gunship; also clears boarders.

TUNING AND DEBUGGING
Tune Assets/HolyHelicopter/Resources/MidnightBalance.asset in Unity's Inspector.
It controls invasion rates, assaults, rescue/hook tolerances, civilian behavior,
fuel, salvage rewards, purchase prices and supply values.
F3 telemetry   F6 +1,000 monsters   F7 +500 salvage   F8 return/service
F9 screenshot in the player's persistent data directory.
Default horde cap: 6,000. Batched sprites and fixed effect pools are preserved.

OPT-IN VALIDATION (NOT NORMAL PLAY)
-holy-smoke-test -holy-report "<absolute report.json>"
-holy-smoke-test -holy-stress validates a controlled 3,000-monster combat sample.
-holy-balance-test -holy-report "<absolute report.json>" runs two five-minute worlds.
-holy-mission-test -holy-report "<absolute report.json>" flies a scripted mission.
-holy-ui-test -holy-ui-directory "<absolute directory>" captures four UI resolutions.
Use -batchmode -nographics for gameplay-only tests, never for screenshots/FPS.

PROTOTYPE SCOPE
One repeatable mission and session upgrades; no saved campaign.
Civilian movement uses lightweight avoidance, not full building navigation.
One skeleton tower operates at a time. Scenery has no general collision or
universal destruction; terrain avoidance and crypt hit volumes remain analytic.
All sprites, scenery, UI and sound are original procedural placeholder assets.
