HOLY HELICOPTER - OPERATION MIDNIGHT MASS

LAUNCH
Run Builds/HolyHelicopter/Holy Helicopter.exe for the Windows prototype.
Keep the executable and its accompanying build files together.
Open Assets/Scenes/HolyHelicopter.unity in Unity 6000.4.1f1 and press Play.
The Holy Helicopter menu also opens the scene and builds a Windows player.
The world, monster art, cockpit graphics and sound are generated locally at runtime.
The original SampleScene is preserved; HolyHelicopter is the enabled build scene.

YOUR FIRST SORTIE
Press Enter or click BEGIN THE EXORCISM. Fly north into the valley.
Silver minigun is on LMB; holy water is on RMB. Water clears crowds.
Destroy the four purple crypts. Each permanently stops that site's spawning.
Rescue at least four of the eight cyan-marked civilians and deliver them home.
Hover below 30 feet, within 8 metres, and nearly stationary to winch a civilian.
You have four passenger seats. Return to the gold Saint Rotor helipad to unload.
Hover below 36 feet and slow down over the pad for free repairs, fuel and ammo.
Press Tab there to open the armory. Buy rockets early: they are ridiculous.
Finish both objectives and return to the pad to complete the operation.

CONTROLS
WASD         Move relative to the helicopter's heading
Mouse        Turn / aim vertically (aim is independent of body tilt)
Q / E        Descend / ascend
Wheel        Change held altitude in small steps
LMB / RMB    Fire assigned weapons (both can fire together)
Tab          Free cursor / return to flight
Left-click a weapon icon to bind LMB; right-click it to bind RMB
Escape       Pause; sensitivity, invert-Y, audio controls
H            Field manual
M            Mute / unmute
R            Restart after victory or defeat
F            Reserved

STAYING ALIVE
Moving or climbing shakes skeleton boarders off. Hover over a large crowd and
the skeletons will build a tower up to you. Shoot the tower or fly away.
Altitude protects against ground melee, but bats and necromancers can reach you.
Below 33 feet, fly slowly over supplies to hook them automatically.
Fuel/repair/full-ammo crates stay put when they are not needed.
Ammo crates refill only weapons you own. Enemy drops are limited to avoid clutter.
Your shots can harm civilians. Rescue them before prolonged village sieges do.
Base defenses protect the immediate helipad; routine base babysitting is unnecessary.
Salvage comes from kills, crypts, deliveries and purple crates. Five weapon levels.

WEAPONS
Silver: precise rapid fire, extra damage to vampires and bats.
Holy water: broad splash, extra damage to skeletons and zombies.
Blessed rockets: traveling explosive crowd/crypt killers; dangerous to civilians.
UV absolution: sustained violet beam, very effective on vampires and bats.
The Rapture: huge ground shockwave beneath the helicopter; clears boarders too.

DEVELOPMENT
F3 telemetry   F6 +1,000 monsters   F7 +500 salvage   F8 return/service
F9 screenshot (saved under the player's persistent data directory).
Initial population: 1,600. Default population cap: 6,000.
Monsters use dense arrays and batched animated sprites, no individual GameObjects.
Scenery is batched mesh geometry; effects and weapon trails have fixed-size pools.
Runtime smoke tests are opt-in: launch the player with -holy-smoke-test
-holy-report "<absolute report.json>". Add -holy-preview for screenshots only.
Use -batchmode -nographics for headless gameplay tests. Add -holy-stress for a
3,000-enemy rendered stress sample. Never use these flags for normal play.

PROTOTYPE SCOPE
One complete mission with upgrades during the mission; no saved campaign yet.
Enemy navigation, village siege damage and boarding are deliberately abstract.
One tower is simulated at a time. Scenery has no general collision/destruction;
terrain avoidance uses analytic height queries and crypts have aiming volumes.
Sprites, scenery, weapons, sound and UI are original procedural placeholders.
No paid assets, services or additional packages are required.
